Does this event work on all platforms?
The event hub will be available on mobile, PC, and console. Some experiences may only be available on certain platforms. You might need to grab another device type if you have run out of quests on your device. Some users may be unable to complete specific quests due to restrictions based on parental controls or platform requirements.
How old do I have to be to join the event?
Most experiences in this event have experience guideline age recommendations of All Ages or Ages 9+. Users with parental controls may not be able to access all experiences. Some users with certain account settings may be unable to access specific quests.
How do I report a bug or issue?
Fill out our support form, and we’ll do our best to get it fixed. Choose from the following categories based on your issue:
  •  • Bug report > Bug report: Experiencing bugs with the event hub or a participating experience.
  •  • Ideas & Suggestions > Feedback: Suggestions of how to improve the event.
  •  • Contests & Events > Prize Not Received: Issues with receiving an item after completing the required number of quests.
  •  • Contests & Events > Question or Issue: General questions with the event.
    • I completed a quest but I didn't get a token. What should I do?
    If you didn’t receive a token, you may not have completed the corresponding quest. Go back to the quest and ensure you’ve completed the necessary steps.
    If you believe you are experiencing a bug preventing you from receiving your token, you can fill out a support form and select: Contests & Events > Prize Not Received.
